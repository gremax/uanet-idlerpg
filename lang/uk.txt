# Ukrainian messages for the IdleRPG bot. Format and placeholders: see en.txt.
#
# Style: the bot addresses the player as «ти». The player's gender is unknown,
# so verbs are in the present tense or impersonal («падає», «додано»), never
# a gendered past tense («упав/упала»). Command names and their keyword
# arguments stay in Latin, as in the Atheme translation
# (docs/atheme-uk-glossary.md in uanet-infra).
